# Day 20: The Most Valuable Thing a Manager Can Give Is Time

The most valuable thing a manager can give you isn't advice.

It's their time.

A manager's calendar is one of the clearest reflections of their priorities.

Mine is often packed too. But I deliberately protect time for 1:1s.

Not because every conversation needs to solve a problem.

But because regular 1:1s send a simple message:

**You matter. I have time for you.**

With a small team, regular 1:1s are easier to manage. With a larger team, you might need to adjust the frequency.

But less frequent doesn't have to mean less valuable.

And there's something else I think is important:

A 1:1 is not a one-way street.

It's not only for your manager to give feedback, assign tasks or check progress.

You can manage up, too.

- Bring a challenge.
- Ask for context.
- Share an idea.
- Talk about your development.
- Ask for what you need.

Your manager's time is valuable.

So is yours.

How do you use your 1:1s?
