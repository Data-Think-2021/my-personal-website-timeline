# Day 5: Why I Publish Between 10pm and Midnight

You might have noticed: my last posts are always published between 10pm and midnight.

Here is why:

- **5:30am** — get up, prepare breakfast
- **6:00am** — wake up my son
- **6:30am** — wake up my daughter
- **7:30am** — bring my daughter to school & walk the dog
- **8:15am** — start work
- **4:30pm** — finish work, drive the kids to their hobbies, walk the dog
- **6:00pm** — cook dinner, eat together, everyone shares their day
- **7:00pm** — dishes, help homework, walk the dog
- **8:00pm** — read with my daughter and stay with her until she falls asleep
- **9:00pm** — finally, Feierabend. Now I can write.
- **10:00pm** — publish.

To be honest, at 8pm, lying next to my daughter after a full day, I could easily fall asleep too. Some evenings I almost do.

What gets me up is not discipline. It's that I said "100 days" publicly. Announcing it here made it a commitment I didn't want to break.

Writing at 9pm became a habit, like walking my dog.

Many people keep asking me how to find time. I don't have more energy than anyone else. Two things help me: mindset and habit make a difference.

Respect to every parent who gives their best to their family all day and still keeps something for themselves at 9pm.

What has helped you stick to a personal goal when you were too tired to feel motivated?
