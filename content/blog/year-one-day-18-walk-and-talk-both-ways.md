# Day 18: Walk & Talk Goes Both Ways

Giving back sounds like a big thing. Mentorship sounds like a program. With the "walk & talk" format it's really simple. Just a phone call, like two old friends. Leadership is not only about having a lot of successful projects and great impact, but also about helping each individual.

Thank you, Hamza Ayaz — I took something home too: your speed, structure and reliability impressed me. The learning went both ways.
