# Day 10: One Year as a Leader — Three Questions to Ask First

One year ago today, I started my first leadership position.

If you're thinking about becoming a manager: I won't tell you to do it. I'll tell you what to ask yourself first.

My honest summary after 365 days is this: the challenges are different from anything I knew as an engineer. And I like the job.

### 1. Do you like working with people — and keeping things running behind the scenes?

Most of my work this year was invisible: preparing meetings and workshops, prioritizing, defining goals. One feedback I received that stayed with me: things run smoothly because of work nobody sees.

And some conversations this year were harder than any technical problem I've solved.

### 2. Can you shut down from work?

I couldn't always. I had sleepless nights this year — waking up and not being able to stop thinking about a work topic. Not resting well is exhausting. I'm still learning this one.

### 3. Can you accept doing less technical work?

Your hands-on work will dramatically decrease. You're there to make sure others can work well. For someone who became a leader because of loving the technical work, this might be the hardest question of the three.

One year in, I am still learning most days.

If you made the move into leadership — which of these three was hardest for you?
