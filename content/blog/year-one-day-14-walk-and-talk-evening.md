# Day 14: Walk & Talk for Night Owls Too

"I'm not an early bird."
A few of you wrote me this after Day 11, where I shared my Walk & Talk offer: free calls during my morning dog walk, for anyone who wants advice, a sounding board, or just someone to listen.

Fair point. So here is the second slot: I also walk my dog in the late afternoon, between 17:00 and 18:30. Night owls, this one is yours.

This morning I had a call with Suresh Amgoth, who later wrote about his experience. Thank you, Suresh, the positive message motivates me to continue.

The idea behind Walk & Talk is simple. My spare time is limited, but the dog walks happen anyway. So I use them to help as many people as I can.

Whatever you're carrying right now: you are not alone.

The hardest part is taking the first step and reaching out.
Morning or evening — the walk is happening either way.
