# Day 1: 100 Days of Year One Begins

I will start another 100 days challenge. **#Day 1** starts now. 🚀

Many of you liked the first 100 days series as Head of Data and Cloud. I documented the journey here on LinkedIn — the challenges, actions, mistakes, leadership lessons, and everything in between.

As my one-year anniversary approaches, many things happened and I thought to start a new series:
**100 Days of Year One.**

For the next 100 days, I'll look back at my first year as a leader and share what I've learned about:

- Expectations vs. reality
- Difficult conversations & conflict
- Hiring, termination & team dynamics
- Performance review & motivation
- Balance work, side projects and as a mom
- Learning from my children help me lead better
- How I failed to initiate data strategy
- How I missed opportunities
- Role model, the power & rules
- .....

Because one year gives me a very different perspective than the first 100 days.

To make it not be a one-way story, I appreciate your comments and perspective.

Follow me and see you next 100 days! ⭐
