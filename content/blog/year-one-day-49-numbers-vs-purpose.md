# Day 49: When Numbers Matter Less Than Purpose

I analysed the numbers behind my first 48 posts. 📊 I was a bit disappointed at first. The results weren't what I had hoped for.

The numbers in the dashboard tell me:

- Posting every day did not perform better.
- Weeks with 2–3 good posts did better than weeks with daily posts.
- My engagement rate went down week after week.
- Weekend posts performed worst.

As a data person, my first instinct was to optimise. Post less. Choose better days. Improve the numbers.
Then I asked myself why I started this series in the first place.

Reach was part of it, but not the main reason. I wanted to document what I'm learning in my first year as a leader. Writing things down has become a habit I enjoy. Maybe one day these reflections will even become a book. 😄

I could use bigger hooks and chase impressions. It's not my style. I'd rather tell my story the way it happened. That's also why I don't use hooks like "In 50 days I got 1 million impressions."

So I've stopped forcing myself to post every day. I write when I have something worth sharing. And when I skip a weekend, I feel relieved, not guilty.

I'll keep analysing the numbers. I just won't let them decide why I write.

Have you ever stopped optimising something because you realised the original reason mattered more than the numbers?
