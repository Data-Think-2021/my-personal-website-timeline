# Day 17: You Have to Be a Role Model

For a while, I came to the office only once a week.

Our hybrid policy says 40% office. I live outside Berlin, the kids are small, the dog and cats want company. No one controlled me. So I told myself it was fine.

Then I noticed a problem: on our fixed team day, not everyone was showing up either.

I didn't want to be unpleasant, so I raised it kindly in 1:1s. Little changed.

Until a peer colleague told me directly: "You have to be a role model. This is important."

That's when it hit me. I was asking my team to follow a rule I wasn't following myself. Of course my words had no weight.

So I brought it to our team weekly. I admitted openly that I hadn't kept to the rule, and that this changes now — starting with me.

Since then, team day is team day again. Not because I asked harder. Because I showed up.

Have you ever caught yourself expecting something from your team that you weren't doing yourself?
